# Professional Statement

## My Values
I believe that security is a responsibility — not just a technical function. I am committed to 
integrity, continuous learning, and a proactive approach to protecting the people and organizations 
that depend on secure systems. I hold myself to high standards of ethical conduct, recognizing 
that the work of a cybersecurity analyst directly impacts the safety and trust of others.

## My Strengths
I bring a strong technical foundation in penetration testing, SIEM tooling, network security, 
Linux systems, and SQL. As a software engineer with a B.Tech in Information Technology, I am 
able to bridge the gap between development and security — understanding systems from both a 
builder's and a defender's perspective. I am analytical, detail-oriented, and capable of 
working methodically through complex security challenges.

## My Interest in Cybersecurity
My interest in cybersecurity is rooted in a genuine desire to protect organizations from cyber 
threats. The constantly evolving threat landscape keeps me engaged and motivated — every new 
attack vector, vulnerability, or incident is an opportunity to learn and improve. I am drawn to 
the investigative nature of security work, particularly threat detection, log analysis, and 
incident response.

## My Professional Goals
My goal is to grow into a skilled cybersecurity analyst capable of detecting, investigating, and 
responding to security incidents with precision and confidence. I am currently advancing my 
expertise through the Google Cybersecurity Certificate program and aim to contribute meaningfully 
to a security team that values both technical excellence and a strong security culture.
