# Course 08 – Put It to Work: Prepare for Cybersecurity Jobs

## Overview

This final course focused on preparing for a career in cybersecurity. Topics included understanding the job landscape, communicating with stakeholders, escalating incidents, and developing professional documents such as a resume and elevator pitch.

## Key Topics

- The role of a security analyst across different organisations
- Incident escalation and stakeholder communication
- Security mindset and professional ethics
- Job search strategies for cybersecurity roles
- Résumé writing and the job interview process

## Skills Gained

- Communicating security concepts to technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Applying a security-oriented mindset to everyday decisions
- Preparing professional career documents for the cybersecurity job market
